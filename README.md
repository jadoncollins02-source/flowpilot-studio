# FlowPilot Studio — website starter

A responsive static website for a proposed small-business digital services studio.

## Proposed offer
Start with a **Lead Capture & Follow-up Setup** service for US small businesses. The website also presents simple websites and workflow/AI prototypes as adjacent services.

This is a market hypothesis to validate, not a claim that customer demand or revenue is guaranteed.

## Included
- Responsive homepage and service cards
- Enquiry form that prepares an email (does not store submissions)
- Draft privacy notice and terms page
- No paid libraries or server-side code
- No active payment gateway (intentionally)

## Before publishing
1. Confirm the business name is available and does not infringe another brand.
2. Replace `REPLACE-WITH-YOUR-EMAIL@example.com` in `script.js` with a real inbox you control.
3. Update all business details and service descriptions to match what you can deliver.
4. Rewrite the privacy notice and terms for your actual business and tools.
5. Connect a secure form service if you want submissions collected without relying on the visitor's email app.
6. Do not add live payment buttons until a payment provider has approved your actual business, country, and service category.
7. Add a clear proposal, scope, cancellation/refund terms, and invoicing process before taking payment.
8. Test mobile layouts and the form on a phone and desktop.

## Free hosting
This is a static HTML/CSS/JS site and can be hosted on GitHub Pages or another static host. For GitHub Pages, create a repository, upload these files, then enable Pages in repository settings. Use the host's current official instructions.

## Current limitations
- No backend, database, user accounts, analytics, or payment integration.
- The contact form opens the visitor's email client after you configure your address.
- The privacy and terms pages are drafts and not legal advice.
- “FlowPilot Studio” is a placeholder brand name; availability has not been checked.
