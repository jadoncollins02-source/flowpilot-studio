# FlowPilot Studio — Version 3

A responsive, dark-colorful static website for a digital services studio.

## What's included
- New midnight-navy theme with violet, cyan, mint, and pink accents.
- Service catalogue for Telegram bots, AI/support chatbots, Discord/community bots, business websites, workflow automation, API integrations, lead capture systems, and custom scripts/tools.
- Live client-side service search. If no listed service matches, the visitor sees a custom-request option.
- Customer contact email: `flowpilotstudio0@gmail.com`.
- Enquiry form that prepares an email draft addressed to the business inbox.
- Privacy notice and terms draft pages updated to match the theme.
- Mobile navigation and responsive layouts.

## Publish on GitHub Pages
1. Download and extract this ZIP.
2. Open your existing `flowpilot-studio` GitHub repository.
3. Upload/replace the six files (`index.html`, `styles.css`, `script.js`, `privacy.html`, `terms.html`, and `README.md`) in the repository root. Do not upload the containing folder as a nested folder.
4. Commit the changes. GitHub Pages should redeploy automatically if it is already enabled.
5. Open the published site and test the service search and enquiry form on your phone.

## Important limitations
- The search filters the service catalogue; it does not automatically fulfill arbitrary searches. Visitors can submit a custom request, and you must confirm feasibility before promising delivery.
- The form opens the visitor's email application. It does not automatically send, store, or notify you of an enquiry unless the visitor reviews and sends the draft.
- No backend, database, login, payment processing, analytics, or automated fulfilment is included.
- Privacy and terms pages are drafts, not legal advice. Customize them to match your actual business and legal obligations.
- The listed services should only be offered when you can deliver them. Confirm scope, pricing, timeline, and third-party costs before accepting work.
- “FlowPilot Studio” brand and domain availability have not been verified.
